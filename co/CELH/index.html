To hit $3.345B for the full year, Q2-Q4 combined must deliver $2.562B, or an average of ~$854M per quarter. The prior-year Q2-Q4 2025 actuals from the historical table are: Q2 2025 $739M, Q3 2025 $725M, Q4 2025 $722M — a combined $2.186B. So the street is modeling Q2-Q4 2026 at roughly $376M above the prior-year Q2-Q4 run-rate, or +17% YoY on those three quarters. A same-period prior-year sequential comparison (Q1 2025 vs. Q4 2024) is not available because Q4 2024 does not appear in our 24-quarter historicals table — the table moves directly from Q3 2024 ($266M) to Q1 2025 ($329M) — so no prior-year seasonal benchmark can be computed from available data. The revenue consensus is directionally achievable but back-end loaded.

The structured financials show FY2025 GAAP gross margin of 50.4% ($1,267.3M gross profit on $2,515.3M revenue). The Rockstar integration is guided for completion in 1H 2026 (deck p.13), which should remove integration cost drag in 2H 2026. However, the tariff headwind is structural — PPI running +13.1% YoY (FRED, as of May 2026) is a real input cost pressure that management has not quantified in dollar terms.

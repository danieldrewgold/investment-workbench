The core growth thesis is that InP—described as a 'growth engine ignited in 2014'—is riding a wave of expanding applications including 'AI / data center expansion, industrial lasers, LED lighting and display, laser-based sensing, wireless devices,' while structural barriers ('crystal growth very difficult,' 'customer spec-driven market,' 'limited number of competitors') protect AXT's position. Management further argues that 'new facilities, equipment and capacity' combined with a potential STAR Market listing of subsidiary Tongmei—which 'can finance more growth and capacity'—will allow AXT to scale into this demand. The narrative is anchored in the combination of proprietary process technology, unique supply chain control, and alignment with secular technology trends.

what is this?

A credit-market early-warning check. Bondholders get paid before shareholders, so the credit market often senses trouble (or comfort) before the stock does. We look at (1) the company's own bonds and whether their spreads are widening, and (2) the broad corporate-bond tape (are investors net buying or selling credit, i.e. risk-on vs risk-off). Spreads blowing out while the stock holds up is a warning; a calm credit tape is reassuring.

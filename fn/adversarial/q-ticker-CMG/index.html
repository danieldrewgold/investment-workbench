counter_evidence
The analyst's own inputs do not reconcile to $1.32. (1) Comp is 1.79% (price 2.0 + transactions 0.39 - mix 0.6) against a stated consensus of ~3.7%. Subtract ~150bp of margin on a ~15% operating base and operating income is roughly flat. Add buybacks and EPS grows low single digits, which lands near $1.17–1.20, not $1.32 (+16%). Either the cost inputs are not what they appear, or $1.32 is far too high. In both cases, the stated $0.05 gap is not the output of the stated drivers. The pitch does not challenge unit economics, Chipotlane mix (~80%) or the 8–10% unit-growth algorithm, which is where a structural short would have to live.

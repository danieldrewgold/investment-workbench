# Session Summary — 2026-05-04 / 2026-05-05

Two-day session that took the workbench from a 7-step DAG with imperative post-fetch logic to a 23-step DAG with structured ownership signals, retail-dialogue analysis, claim verification, and per-paragraph inline research sub-bullets. ## What's now in the pipeline

23-step DAG: 14 parallel fetches → 1 transcript_digest → 2 parallel (slide_decks, social_topic_analysis) → 1 guidance_bundle → 1 corpus_assembly → 1 research_brief → 1 claim_verifications.

The fetch wave runs all of these in parallel:
financials, filing_text, transcripts, consensus, market_overlay, press_releases, fred_macro, bls_macro, bea_macro, peer_comps, quarterly_financials, news, bear_research, **crowding_assessment**, **filing_13d**, **stocktwits**.

Then assembly: corpus_assembly → research_brief (the central Claude call, content-hash cached on all inputs + a `_BRIEF_PROMPT_VERSION` constant) → claim_verifications.

## Crowding refinements

- Distinguishes `latest_filed_quarter` (may be in-flight) from `fully_reported_quarter` (>= 50% universe filed). Trend metrics use the latter.
- Top-5 buyers + top-5 sellers based on per-fund $-delta over the fully-reported quarter. Surfaces the actual disagreement (e.g. quant vs fundamental long-only) rather than a flat "X of N hold" count.
- In-flight quarter flagged in caveats so the brief doesn't misread early-filer signal.

## Social topic analyzer

Critical new layer. Single Sonnet call takes the StockTwits stream and the transcripts, returns themes with `silence_flag` markers. Validated on TMDX:

- 4 [SILENCE] themes correctly flagged: margin compression (8 msgs), oil/supply-chain costs (3 msgs), CEO leadership concerns (4 msgs), massive EPS miss (6 msgs).
- 2 [ADDRESSED] themes with verbatim transcript snippets: heart revenue deceleration, European expansion.
- 1 neutral retail theme: buying opportunity at current levels.

The TMDX Q1 2026 print (May 5) confirmed margin compression + supply-chain costs as real, validating the silence-flag mechanism retrospectively.

## Brief / EPS bridge / edge detector guardrails

- Brief abort: hard error only when `driver_count < 2 AND n_drivers < 2 AND generic_schema`. PRMB-style hollow briefs abort; APP-style 1-driver-multi-component briefs continue with warnings.
- Schema mismatch warning: fires when peer comps inferred a sector schema but brief picked `general`.
- Bridge fallback diagnostic: `BaselinePnL.validity_reason` records exactly why is_valid() failed; surfaces in `brief_warnings`. Bridge now accepts partial baseline (uses prior-year revenue × growth when consensus revenue is missing).
- Edge detector downgrade: post-process the verdict to `MODELING_FAILURE` when bridge fell back AND `variant_pct > 50%` AND drivers are thin. Prevents fitting noise on broken inputs.
- `--force` flag: bypasses the thin-brief abort. Used for synthesis-focused reports on names like TMDX where Claude consistently produces rich prose but few structured drivers.

## Known issues for next session

- **Thin-brief problem on TMDX, PRMB, IBRX-style names** — brief Claude call consistently returns 1 driver / generic schema for sectors without a `sector_drivers` schema match (med devices, beverage roll-ups, biotech). Workarounds: `--force` flag bypasses; `registry_data.schema` hint helps. Real fix: add med-device + beverage schemas to `DRIVER_REGISTRY`.
- **Vanguard "internal restructure" anomaly** — Q3 2025 holdings show 7,756 shares (vs ~3.5M before/after). Per-fund delta calc shows +46,350% Q3→Q4 which is an artifact of the anomaly. Should detect step-function changes and label them.
- **13D share-count regex** — works for cleanly-formatted SC 13D primary docs but cover-page extraction is fragile. Currently captures Item 11 "Aggregate Amount Beneficially Owned" via regex; some filings use unusual layouts that break it.
- **StockTwits sentiment sparseness** — labels are user-applied; even on TMDX with 196 messages, 9 bull / 1 bear / 186 unlabeled. The social_topic_analyzer's `sentiment_lean` per theme is more useful.
- **Brief doesn't always cite [SILENCE] flags directly** — the corpus block makes them visible, but Claude sometimes integrates obliquely (which is the design) and sometimes doesn't surface the flag at all. Could be improved with explicit prompt guidance on how to handle silence flags.
- **Q1 2026 in-flight caveat** — only 4/47 funds have filed Q1 2026 13Fs as of today. Re-run `refresh-13f` after May 14 deadline to get the full Q1 picture.

# CMG: NO EDGE (low conviction)
**$30.77** (close 2026-10-07) | probability-weighted value **$32.22** (+4.7%)

**Thesis.** At $30.77, CMG trades close to our base case and our probability-weighted value is only 4.7% above the price, so we have no edge (low conviction); the Street's $1.37 FY2027 EPS rests on a restaurant margin recovery that is still only a management promise.

**Where we differ from consensus.**
- Comparable sales: ours 3.10% vs 6.39% needed for consensus EPS. Our base comp of +3.1% is only modestly below the roughly 3.7% that consensus revenue implies, so comp is not where most of the EPS gap lives. Traffic alone would need +3.8% to reach the $1.37 consensus, outside our -1.5% to +2.0% range. That tells us the Street is not really underwriting a traffic boom either. Our +0.5% base traffic sits below Q2's +1.0% transaction comp. Management guided Q3 comps to about +1% with price in the mid-2% range, which implies negative traffic plus mix. Management also admitted trends softened in late July. Our +2.6% check reflects the mid-2% Q3 pricing guide and the promise that price matches inflation from Q4, not price running ahead of it.
- Restaurant margin: ours 23.30% vs 24.54% needed for consensus EPS. This is where the gap to consensus sits. Our base restaurant margin of 23.30% is about 26bp above the 23.04% FY2026E base year. The Street needs 24.54%, which is almost exactly our bull case of 24.58%. Management's own statements against interest limit the recovery. Labor hours saved by the new equipment are being reinvested, and Q2 labor rose partly on hospitality spending. The formal guide says only that price will match inflation from Q4, which stops the roughly 150bp dislocation from widening but does not reverse it. The promises to win that margin back ([S20] 'temporary and will be recovered', [S53] 'we'll get that back by the end of the year') are self-serving narrative. Q2 margin was still down 220bp, so they remain untested.

**What would change the stance.** On price, with the cases held fixed: long at or below $28.01, where the $32.22 weighted value is 15% or more above the price; avoid above $32.22, where the weighted value is below the price and the $22.72 bear case is 25% or more below it; short at or above $37.91, where the weighted value is 15% or more below the price. Q3 2026 restaurant margin is the main swing factor because the gap to consensus is a margin question. A decline of 100bp or less from Q3 2025's 24.5% would show recovery running ahead of our base and would raise the bull weight. A decline as large as H1's 220bp to 250bp would support the base-year path and the bear tail. A Q3 transaction comp below -1.0% would push traffic toward the bear's -1.5% and raise that case's probability. A comp of 2.5% or better with positive transactions would move weight toward bull. Any sign that Q4 pricing falls short of cost inflation would break the formal promise behind the base check of +2.6%. A first FY2027 comp guide of mid single digits would meet the condition attached to the 40% flow-through claim, while low single digits would leave that claim untested. FY2027 adjusted G&A growth guided above 9% would match the bear assumption.

**Catalysts.**
- 2026-10-28: Q3 2026 results
- 2027-02-02: Q4 2026 results and first FY2027 guide (date not yet set; early February in prior years)

**Kill criteria.**
- Q3 2026 restaurant margin at or above 23.5% (a decline of 100bp or less) with a comp of 2.5% or more: raise the bull probability.
- Q3 2026 transaction comp below -1.0%: raise the bear probability, since traffic would be tracking the bear's -1.5%.
- Q4 2026 pricing disclosed below cost inflation: the formal promise [S89] behind the base +2.6% check would be broken.

**Main risk.** Price matching inflation from Q4, roughly 2,000 equipment-package stores by year-end, and an easy Q4 2025 compare could together deliver mid single-digit comps. That would trigger the 40% flow-through claim and take margin to the bull's 24.58% and EPS to $1.44. On a multiple at the 8th percentile of its own history, that case is worth +50%. We hold it at 20% because it relies on the untested promises [S20], [S53] and [S27].

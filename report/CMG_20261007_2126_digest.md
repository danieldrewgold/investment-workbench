## Kill criteria
- Two consecutive quarters of transaction comp at or above +2.0% (the bull-case traffic) would invalidate our +0.5% base traffic and the no edge stance.
- A Q4 2026 restaurant margin at or above the Q4 2025 level of 23.4% would show the dislocation reversing, not just halting, because the prior-year figure included a 70bp one-off. That would support the bull margin of 24.58%.
- A transaction comp at or below -1.5% (bear-case traffic) in Q3 or Q4 2026 would make the 25% bear weight too low.
- A FY2027 guide implying adjusted G&A growth of 9% or more, or a share count reduction below 3%, would push EPS toward the $1.03 bear case.

## Strongest counter
The bull case is closer than its 20% weight implies. Traffic improved in each of the last two quarters, to +1.0% in Q2. Price stops lagging inflation from Q4. The next two prints lap the weakest 2025 compares, including a -2.5% Q4 base, and management has met or beaten near-term guides 11 of 11 times. If price recovery plus kept equipment efficiency lifts margin to around 24.5%, EPS approaches $1.44. At the 32x bull multiple, that is +50%. The rebuttal is that a 24.5% margin is exactly the long-dated recovery promise that remains untested, and the price already gives no credit for it.

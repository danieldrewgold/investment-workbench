# CMG: NO EDGE (medium conviction)
**$30.90** (close 2026-10-06) | expected value **$33.28** (+7.7%)

**Thesis.** At $30.90 CMG trades at about 23.6x our FY2027 EPS of $1.31, which already prices a partial margin recovery and no re-rating, so our probability-weighted value of about $33.30 (+8%) falls short of the 15% hurdle in either direction.

**What would create an edge.** A long opens below about $29.00, where EV clears +15%. Above $29.00, a long also opens if the 2026-10-28 Q3 print shows labor as a percent of sales flat or down YoY and a Q4 restaurant-margin guide flat or up YoY excluding the $27M gift card true-up. A short opens only above about $39 with no improvement in the margin data, or if the Q3 comp prints below 0% and the Q4 restaurant margin is guided down 150bp or more YoY.

**Where we differ.**
- Restaurant-level margin FY2027: ours 23.80 vs consensus 24.35 %
- Comparable restaurant sales growth FY2027: ours 2.50 vs consensus 3.75 %

**The multiple.** 22.6x next-FY (FY2027) consensus P/E looks fair, likely to hold. At 22.6x consensus FY2027 EPS and 23.6x ours, the market is paying a trailing-equivalent multiple near 30x for a business whose EPS did not grow in 2026. In 2025 it paid 40.8x for +17% growth. Today's price therefore assumes neither the 19% rebound consensus models nor any re-rating. The market already discounts EPS of about $1.30 and a slow margin recovery.

**Kill criteria.**

- Q4 2026 restaurant margin at or above Q4 2025 excluding the 70bp gift card benefit (22.7% or higher): the dislocation has closed and $1.37 is reachable.
- An initial FY2027 comp guide of mid-single digits, or a Q4 2026 comp of 4% or better: our 2.5% FY2027 comp is wrong.

**Main risk.** The red team argues our FY2026 $1.14 quietly assumes the H2 margin recovery our FY2027 view denies, and that HEEP and buybacks are unmodeled. The stance survives for three reasons. First, on an adjusted basis H2 2026 EPS of about $0.58 against about $0.55 is explained by 5-6% share shrink with roughly flat net income. That fits a slowly narrowing margin gap, not a recovery. Second, management itself says HEEP labor hours are reinvested, not banked. Third, buybacks are in our share count of about 1,190M. The red team is right that a 4% EPS gap is too thin to short a stock at 52-week lows with takeover chatter. That is why the call is no_edge, not short.

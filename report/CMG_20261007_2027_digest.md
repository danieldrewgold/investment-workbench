# CMG: NO EDGE, low conviction
Price $30.90 (close 2026-10-06, Polygon previous-session close). Probability-weighted value $33.49, +8.4% vs price. The stance follows from that against a 15% hurdle. Run 2026-10-07.

## Thesis
CMG at $30.90 is a no-edge, low-conviction call: the probability-weighted value of $33.49 sits only 8.4% above the price, because a 30% bear case worth $23.40 offsets a base case where restaurant margin, not traffic, leaves our FY2027 EPS about 6% below the Street.

## Management read
- Near-term guides (given within 4 months of the period end) are usually beaten or met: 11 of 11 came in at or better than guided.
- In-year updates (4 to 9 months out): 10 of 12 at or better than guided.
- Long-dated guides on items management controls (openings, tax, capex): 6 of 6 delivered.
- Long-dated demand guides and promises (9+ months out) are where management misses: 1 of 2 missed. Comparable / same-store sales growth FY2025 guided 2 to 5, actual -1.7 (-5.2pp).
- Open long-dated promises (not yet testable under their own conditions): [S07] The new equipment package will produce a step change in throughput; [S11] Chipotle can reach 7,000 North American restaurants; [S12] Transaction growth will return and push AUVs above $4M; [S18] Chipotle will come out of the downturn stronger than before; [S20] The margin hit from underpricing inflation is temporary and will be recovered; [S21] Comps can return to mid single digits in 2026.
- **Flow-through:** In Q3 2025, CFO Rymer said Chipotle can 'return back to that ideal 40% flow through over time as we get back to mid single digit comps and are driving transactions again.' The claim is untested, not refuted, because its condition has not held: the best comp so far is +2.2% and transactions are about +1%. Our base comp of +3.4% also falls short of mid single digits, so the base case does not apply 40% flow-through. Only the bull case's margin recovery resembles it, and that case carries 20%.
- **Credibility:** Management is reliable on near-term guides and on items it controls: openings and tax delivered 6 of 6. It is unreliable on long-dated demand promises. The CEO's mid-single-digit 2026 comp ambition (S21) is tracking at low single digits. The Q3 2025 claim that the Q4 guide was conservative (S23) is refuted: Q4 printed -2.5%, inside the guided range. We treat margin-recovery language as hypotheses until margins move.
- Management stopped citing new-restaurant productivity (about 80%) and year-two cash-on-cash returns (about 60%) after Q3 2025. The unit economics behind the 7.0pp of new-unit revenue are no longer independently disclosed.
- A Q2 2026 question on what drives 1% to 2% comps into 2027 was answered with guidance-raise framing, not data. The bull case's traffic assumption therefore lacks independent support.
- Buybacks continued at falling average prices ($42.39, then $34.14, then $32.55). That is a money-backed signal of confidence near today's price.
- Tone moved from defensive to confident over four quarters. That tracks the comp improvement but adds no evidence on margin.

## Kill criteria
- Q3 2026 transaction comp below -1.0%, which would make our +0.6% base traffic too high and require raising the bear probability above 30%.
- Q4 2026 restaurant margin below 22.5%, against 23.4% in Q4 2025 (which included a 70bp gift card benefit). This would show the dislocation widening rather than stopping as S89 guides.
- FY2027 pricing guided more than 1 point below cost inflation, which would invalidate the bull case's margin recovery and our base check of +2.8%.
- Adjusted G&A growth guided above 11% for FY2027. That is the bear-case input, against 8% in our base.
- Two consecutive quarters of transaction comp at +2.0% or better, which would make the bull case's traffic the run rate and its 20% weight too low.

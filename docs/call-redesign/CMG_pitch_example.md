# CMG: NO EDGE (low conviction)
**$30.77** (close 2026-10-07) | probability-weighted value **$32.22** (+4.7%)

**Thesis.** At $30.77 CMG prices close to our base case, and the probability-weighted value of $32.22 is only 4.7% above the price, so we have no edge (low conviction): the stock is cheap against its own history but not against the earnings we can underwrite.

**Where we differ from consensus.**
- Restaurant margin: ours 23.30% vs 24.54% needed for consensus EPS. This is where the whole gap to consensus sits. Our base EPS of $1.26 is 8.0% below the Street's $1.37. With our base revenue, the Street's number needs a 24.54% restaurant margin, which is effectively our bull-case margin of 24.58%. Our base margin of 23.30% sits slightly below the FY2026E base year. The Street is therefore underwriting a margin recovery that management has promised but not delivered. Q2 2026 margin was still down 220bp. Management's only commitment from Q4 is that price will match inflation. That stops the dislocation from widening. It does not reverse it.
- Comparable sales: ours 3.10% vs 6.39% needed for consensus EPS. Our base comp of +3.1% is not where the disagreement lies. Traffic alone would need +3.8% to reach consensus. That is outside our whole -1.5% to +2.0% traffic range, so the comp lever cannot close the gap on its own. Our base traffic of +0.5% is deliberately below Q2's +1.0% transaction print. Management has already flagged late-July softening, and its Q3 guide is only about +1% on mid-2% pricing. Both point to flat-to-negative traffic in the near term.

**What would change the stance.** On price, with the cases held fixed: long at or below $28.01, where the $32.22 weighted value is 15% or more above the price; avoid above $32.22, where the weighted value is below the price and the $22.72 bear case is 25% or more below it; short at or above $37.91, where the weighted value is 15% or more below the price. Q3 2026 transaction comp: +1.0% or better against the guided ~+1% total comp would lift the bull probability; -1.5% or worse would lift the bear. Q3 restaurant margin against 24.5% a year ago: a decline of 100bp or less supports a recovery path toward the 24.54% consensus needs; a decline above 200bp points to the bear's 21.52%. Q4 pricing near 3% that actually matches cost inflation, as promised. A Q3 labor ratio below Q3 2025's 25.2%, which would show equipment savings being kept rather than reinvested. Initial FY2027 comp guide in February: low-to-mid single digits with positive transactions would move weight to bull; flat to 1% would move it to bear. Quarterly buybacks well below the $631M Q2 pace, which would cut the 4% base share reduction.

**Catalysts.**
- 2026-10-28: Q3 2026 results
- 2027-02-03: Q4 2026 results and initial FY2027 guidance (expected early February; exact date not confirmed)

**Kill criteria.**
- Q3 2026 restaurant margin at or above 24.0% (down 50bp or less year over year): raise the bull probability, since the margin recovery consensus needs would be visibly under way.
- Q3 2026 transaction comp at or below -1.5%: raise the bear probability, since that is the bear-case traffic run rate.
- Q4 2026 pricing below 2.5% while management still cites ~4% cost inflation: the promise has failed, so cut the bull case.

**Main risk.** The bull probability may be too low. The consensus margin of 24.54% lies inside our own range. Price matching inflation from Q4 removes the main 2026 drag. The equipment package reaches about 2,000 stores by year-end, and transactions accelerated from +0.6% to +1.0% in H1. If management simply stops reinvesting the labor hours, margin could recover with little help from comp. At 22.5x consensus, the stock would then be cheap. We hold the bull at 20% because the recovery is still a management promise contradicted by its own reinvestment statement, not an observed trend.
